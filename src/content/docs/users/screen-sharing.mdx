Fancy Mumble lets you share what is on your screen with everyone in
your channel. The server fans your single upload out to every viewer,
so it stays smooth even with many people watching.

You can also **draw on top of any stream** with the built-in whiteboard,
which is great for "look at this" moments.

## Start sharing your screen

<Steps>

1. Click the **Screen share** button <Icon name="lucide:monitor-up"
   size={20} /> in the call-control area (bottom of the chat panel,
   or the call bar at the top).

2. Pick what to share:
   - **Entire screen**: everything on the selected monitor.
   - **Window**: a specific application window (private notes stay
     private).
   - **Browser tab** (when running through a browser): one tab only.

3. Confirm. A small indicator appears under your name to show you are
   broadcasting.

4. To stop, click the same button again or use the **Stop sharing**
   bar at the top of the window.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
You can be in a different channel from your viewers and still send to
your home channel. As long as you have permission to broadcast in
that channel, the server will deliver your stream.
</Aside>

## Watching someone share

When anyone in your channel starts streaming, a thumbnail appears in
the **stream grid** between the chat and the member list.

- **Click a thumbnail** to make that stream the focus view (large
  center window).
- **Click the focus view** again to return to the grid.
- **Pop out** with the <Icon name="lucide:picture-in-picture-2"
  size={18} /> button to detach the stream as its own floating
  window. Useful when you want chat on one monitor and the stream on
  another.
- **Resize** the focus view by dragging its corner.

## Multi-stream

Up to a handful of people can broadcast at the same time. Each one
shows as a tile in the grid. The focus view tracks whichever tile was
clicked last.

If your channel uses a *presentation* setup (one presenter, many
viewers) the admin can set a permission so only the presenter may
broadcast. See [Permission flags](/reference/permissions/).

## Drawing on top (whiteboard)

Anyone watching can **draw on top** of the focused stream:

<Steps>
1. Open the focus view of the stream you want to mark up.
2. Click the **pencil** button <Icon name="lucide:pencil" size={18} />
   in the stream toolbar.
3. Pick a color and brush size.
4. Draw with the mouse or with touch. Other viewers see your strokes
   live.
5. Click the <Icon name="lucide:eraser" size={18} /> button to clear
   your own strokes, or the **trash** to clear everyone's strokes (if
   you have permission).
</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
Strokes are tied to the stream, not the channel. When the broadcaster
stops sharing, the strokes are cleared.
</Aside>

## Watch Together

In the same area, the **Watch Together** feature lets one person play
a video URL that is synchronized across the channel. Pause and seek
events propagate to every viewer.

Open it from the **Watch Together** menu next to the screen-share
button.

## Tips

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Share a specific window" icon="laptop">
    Sharing the full desktop is convenient, but a single window keeps
    private windows (calendar, messages, banking) hidden.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Resolution and frame rate" icon="setting">
    The server caps each stream at a reasonable bitrate. Use a smaller
    window for sharper text, or full screen for video.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Audio sharing" icon="seti:audio">
    On Windows and most browsers, the picker offers an **Also share
    system audio** checkbox. Useful for music, games, or video calls.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Privacy first" icon="approve-check">
    The picker is system-provided. Fancy Mumble cannot start a stream
    of something you did not explicitly choose.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## When sharing does not work

- **Black screen** at the viewer: see
  [Screen-share problems](/troubleshooting/screen-share/).
- **Cannot start the stream**: your operating system blocks screen
  capture for the app. Grant permission under system Privacy
  settings.
- **The button is missing**: the server does not have the screen-share
  relay enabled. See
  [Screen sharing relay](/server/features/webrtc-sfu/) for the server
  side.
- **Viewers cannot hear audio**: re-tick the **Also share system
  audio** box when starting the stream.
