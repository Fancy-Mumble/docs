Audio is the number one thing people fiddle with in voice chat. This
page walks every control under **Settings, Voice**, what it does, when
to change it, and the trade-off.


<img src="/screenshot-users-audio-1.png" alt="Voice settings panel showing input/output device dropdowns, activation mode radio buttons (Voice Activation selected), noise suppression algorithm dropdown set to RNNoise, Auto Sensitivity toggle, Current Threshold readout, Calibrate button, and Auto Gain controls" />

<Aside type="tip">
Hidden controls? Some advanced sliders only appear in **Expert mode**.
Turn it on under **Settings, Advanced, Expert Mode**.
</Aside>

## Input and output devices

Two side-by-side dropdowns at the top of the panel:

- **Input Device** is your microphone. *System default* picks whatever
  the operating system is currently using. Pick a specific device by
  name if you have multiple mics.
- **Output Device** is where audio is played. Same logic.

Each has a **volume slider** from 0% to 200%. Past 100% the app
amplifies digitally, so it can clip if pushed too high.

<Aside type="caution">
Some USB headsets expose two output endpoints (game and chat). Pick
the *game* or *handset* endpoint for Mumble. The *chat* endpoint is
mono and narrow-band on some products.
</Aside>

## Activation mode

Three radio buttons decide **when you transmit**:

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Voice Activation" icon="seti:audio">
    Mic transmits when speech is detected. Threshold driven, default.
    The noise removal runs on the input.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Continuous" icon="error">
    Always transmit. No noise removal, no gate. Use only in studio
    rooms or for recording.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Push to Talk" icon="puzzle">
    Transmit only while a key is held. Pick the key with the **PTT
    key** recorder below.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

### PTT key recording

When push to talk is selected a **shortcut recorder** appears. Click
it, press the key combination you want, then release. Most modifier
combos work (`Ctrl+Alt+Z`, mouse side buttons, single keys, and more).
The key works globally, even when the app is not focused.

## Noise removal

Available **only in Voice Activation mode**. Push-to-talk and
Continuous bypass the noise remover. Pick from a dropdown:

| Algorithm | Speed | Quality | Best for |
|-----------|:-----:|:-------:|----------|
| **DeepFilterNet** | Heavy | Best | Modern computers, keyboard or family noise |
| **RNNoise** | Light | Very good | Most setups, default |
| **OMLSA-IMCRA** | Medium | Good | Smooth output, AC hum |
| **Spectral subtraction** | Very light | Basic | Steady backgrounds |
| **None** | n/a | n/a | Studio mic in a quiet room |

You can **switch live**. Test by speaking while a steady noise source
is running (fan, AC) and compare.

<Aside type="note">
Algorithms that were not compiled into your build will not appear in
the dropdown.
</Aside>

### Expert: per-algorithm controls

In Expert mode an **Advanced controls** strip appears for each
algorithm. Common knobs:

- **Attenuation limit**: how much noise the algorithm is allowed to
  remove.
- **Noise floor adapt rate**: how fast the algorithm learns new noises.
- **Smoothing window**: how many frames are averaged for the cleanup.

The defaults are tuned per algorithm. Do not change them unless you
can hear the difference and have time to A/B test.

## Auto sensitivity

A toggle that **continuously re-fits** the voice-activation threshold
to your room. Recommended for most users.


<img src="/screenshot-users-audio-2.png" alt="Calibrate mode active: the level meter shows a colour bar (green to orange) with a vertical threshold marker and the current threshold value at 3.1 dB" />

### Calibrate (live level meter)

Hit **Calibrate** under the threshold slider. Three visual elements
appear:

- **Fill bar**: instant level.
- **Peak indicator**: a small marker that decays over time.
- **Threshold line**: a vertical line at the current threshold.

Speak naturally, the fill should comfortably cross the line. Stay
silent, the fill should drop below. Adjust the threshold (or turn on
auto sensitivity) until both are true.

## Audio Processing, Auto Gain

| Control | Range | Default | Description |
|---------|-------|---------|-------------|
| **Auto Gain** toggle | n/a | On | Pushes quiet speech up to a target loudness. |
| **Max Amplification** | 1 to 40 dB | 20 dB | Ceiling on how much the auto-gain may add. |

A higher max can rescue distant speakers, but also makes background
noise louder. Pair with a stronger noise removal algorithm.

## Compression

| Control | Range | Default | What |
|---------|-------|---------|------|
| **Quality** (bitrate) | 8 to 320 kbps | 56 kbps | Higher is better quality and more bandwidth. |
| **Audio per packet** | 10, 20, 40, 60 ms | 20 ms | Smaller is lower latency and more packets per second. |

<Aside type="tip">
On cellular or shared Wi-Fi, **40 or 60 ms** at **24 to 32 kbps** is
the most resilient. On a wired connection bump to **96 kbps and 20 ms**
for music-quality voice.
</Aside>

## Network

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="UDP (default)" icon="seti:default">
    Voice flows over UDP for minimum latency. This is what you want.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Force TCP" icon="warning">
    Sends all audio through the existing control connection. Adds a
    little latency, but **passes through any firewall that allows the
    initial connect**. Toggle this under **Voice, Network**.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Expert section

Visible only when Expert mode is on.

### Gate Close Ratio (hysteresis)

The gate has two thresholds, open and close. The close threshold is
the open threshold times this ratio (default 0.8). Lower ratio is
more aggressive cut-off, and possibly clips the ends of sentences.

### Hold Frames

Number of frames to keep the gate open after audio drops below the
close threshold. Each frame is the *audio-per-packet* duration.
Increase if your *t/s/p/k* sounds get cut.

### Legacy audio backend <Badge text="Desktop only" variant="caution" />

The default audio backend supports modern Linux (PipeWire and Pulse),
Windows, and macOS. Toggle this to fall back to the legacy backend for
niche setups. Takes effect on the next voice toggle.

## Audio statistics <Badge text="Expert" variant="caution" />

Live packet counters per connection:

```
To Client (server sent):    good   late   lost (n%)   resync
From Client (we received):  good   late   lost (n%)   resync
```

- **good**: packets received in order.
- **late**: out of order, or older than the buffer head.
- **lost**: never arrived.
- **resync**: codec resync events after a long pause.

> Sustained **more than 2% loss** is bad. Try **Force TCP** or a lower
> bitrate.
