Fancy Mumble runs on Android with all of the core features (talking,
chat, screen-share viewing, file sharing, custom emotes) and a few
small adjustments for touch.

The screenshots show the current phone layout with a sample community.

## Install

See the [installer page](/getting-started/install/) and pick the
**Android** tab.

## Permissions

On the first launch Android will ask for:

| Permission | Why | Required? |
|------------|-----|-----------|
| **Microphone** | To send your voice. | Yes, talking does not work without it. |
| **Notifications** | To show push notifications when the app is closed. | Optional, but you will miss messages without it. |
| **Photos and files** | To attach files and images in chat. | Optional, only asked when you tap the paperclip. |

You can revisit these in **Android settings, Apps, Fancy Mumble,
Permissions**.

## Push notifications

If the server supports it (see [Push notifications](/server/features/push/)),
you will get a notification when:

- a new chat message arrives in a channel you follow,
- *(optional)* someone reacts to your message,
- *(optional)* a user joins a channel you watch.

Tap a notification to open the app on that channel.

<Aside type="caution" title="Battery savers">
Some phone makers ship aggressive battery optimizers that delay push
notifications. Open **Settings, Battery, Fancy Mumble** and pick
**Unrestricted** to keep notifications on time.
</Aside>

## Channels and conversations

The **Channels**, **Friends**, and **Settings** tabs sit at the bottom of the navigation screen. Select a channel to open its conversation; use **Back** to return to the channel list. The server-strip control shows or hides saved servers.

<img src="/screenshot-mobile-channels.png" alt="Current mobile channel list with a lounge and five members with distinct avatars" style="width: 100%; max-width: 360px; margin: 0 auto;" />

Chat gives the conversation the whole screen. Its composer stays at the bottom, and an active voice call adds a compact call bar above it. File-sharing controls depend on the connected server's capabilities.

## Call controls

Tap **In voice** on the call bar to expand the call screen. It shows the channel's participants and their audio states. Use the large microphone and headphones controls to mute or deafen, **Disconnect** to leave voice, and the down arrow to return to chat.

<img src="/screenshot-mobile-voice.png" alt="Expanded mobile call screen showing five participants, listening and muted states, microphone and headphones controls, and Disconnect" style="width: 100%; max-width: 360px; margin: 0 auto;" />

## Profiles and message actions

Tap a member's name or avatar to inspect their profile and available actions. Profiles can include their own avatar, banner, status, pronouns, and bio. Long-press a message for its available actions, such as reply, reaction, or copy; moderation actions depend on your permissions.

## Battery and data

- Audio uses around 20 to 96 kbps depending on the **bitrate** slider.
- A one-hour call is about 10 to 40 MB.
- Watching a screen share takes about 1 to 3 Mbps per stream.
- File downloads use whatever the file weighs, once.

Enable **Force TCP audio** in **Settings, Voice** on flaky cell data. It
adds a little latency but avoids packet-loss audio glitches.

## Known mobile limits

- **You cannot broadcast a screen** from a phone yet. You can still
  *view* desktop shares.
- **Whiteboard input** is view-only on mobile.
- **PTT key recording** picks a software button instead of a hardware
  key.
